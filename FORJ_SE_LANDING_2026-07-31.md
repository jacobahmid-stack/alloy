# forj.se landing page brief: the definitive build

## 1. THE ONE-LINE PROMISE (hero)

**Headline (the wound):** You've mastered AWS. Europe is still a wall.

**Promise line (the resolution, the five-second answer):** The European accounts AWS can't hand you, made lawful to reach, so your reps close pipeline you couldn't touch from outside.

**Subhead (the four gaps to the resolution):** From outside Europe you have no local data, no local language, no named buyers, and no way to know which outreach is even lawful market by market. Forj is your side of that wall: net-new European accounts AWS can't give you, the buyer named, the AWS-funded door on each one, and the local rule built into every opener your reps send. You bring the AWS motion. We make Europe reachable, lawfully. Your people close.

Why this hero: all three test-CROs called the wall-and-four-gaps the sharpest thing on the page and said the wound was theirs. It is kept. What changed is the resolution: it now lands on lawful reach (the moat), not on "read by cloud" (the commodity that made the page read as generic enrichment).

## 3. THE THREE ANCHOR PROOFS (uncopyable) AND WHERE EACH LIVES

1. **The lawful-channel wedge (knowledge moat).** A statute-verified map of what outreach is lawful across fourteen European markets, built into what Smith drafts. A partner in Austin or Bangalore literally cannot know Denmark is call-first or that a Norwegian named mailbox is gated while a general one is not. It cannot be scraped or bought; it was built by reading the law. **Lives in Section 3, the first and highest proof, with the live matrix.**

2. **The European library AWS will not give you, read by cloud (data plus method moat).** Net-new accounts discovered from national registers at near-zero cost, each domained account measured (origin, not guessed) for the cloud it runs. AWS gives partners no self-service search of an account universe and never manufactures net-new from a registry; a data vendor sells rows but not the measured cloud and the lawful map on top. The combination is the moat. **Lives in Section 4, with the honest coverage strip and one live read.**

3. **The founder who has already run this exact motion from inside Europe (credibility moat).** Native Swedish, professional English, Nordic AWS partner GTM built from zero, a major AWS partnership co-closed. Uncopyable by definition and, for this ICP, the trust bridge that makes an outsider believe Forj can reach the inside. **Lives in Section 9, positioned as the reason to believe everything above it.**

Everything else (funded-door fluency, the Partner Central read, Smith's drafting) supports. AWS now ships some of it first-party or a rival can template it, so those must never carry the headline.

## 7. THE GUARDRAIL CHECKLIST (a violation kills the page)

**Copy and voice**
- No em dashes anywhere. Use commas, periods, semicolons, colons.
- Buyer language and outcomes only. Never how it is built. Earned, not given; craft, not hustle.

**The overclaim traps the CROs caught (do not reintroduce)**
- Never "the whole European market" and never "335,000 across seven countries, each read by cloud." Belgium is not loaded, Ireland was loaded IT-only in error, France is loading (a shelf), and only about 110,000 accounts are actually cloud-read. Say "net-new accounts you cannot search for anywhere," lead with the wedge, keep any number as supporting and coverage-honest (for example "90,000-plus Nordic accounts already read by cloud"), never as totality.
- Coverage must be honest and up front: Nordics live and worked, France loading, more opening. The fourteen-market lawful map is intentionally wider than the live account library; keep the two separate so Germany can appear on the map without implying named German accounts exist today.
- Cloud-read supports, never headlines. As a headline it reads as generic enrichment and picks an accuracy fight. "No major cloud" means "cloud estate not observable from outside," never "no cloud spend." Publish no cloud-read accuracy percentage. Do not resurrect the old inflated AWS count.
- Cut "before Partner Central can see it" and any swipe at AWS tooling; the reader co-sells through AWS. Use "AWS scores the list you bring it, it never hands you the list."

**Wedge specifics**
- Soften enforcement to "built into what Smith drafts," not "obeys the local rule automatically." The full machine-enforcement field is not wired yet.
- The line "two of the four Nordic markets gate cold email, a call is not" is a dated line. It must be recut the day France and Belgium ship. Flag any market-count sentence as dated.

**Contacts**
- Pitch reachability, not volume. Publish no email or phone coverage percentage (it dates instantly and invites a scrub). Lead the refusal claim (rows we will not stand behind we do not hand you). Do not front-run the register-contact notice duty in copy.

**Funded door**
- "Named where the data allows," not universally. Never claim a dollar total of under-used AWS funding (no primary source). Never imply an open MDF application or that a PDM allocates it. Keep off the program mechanics a PDM checks instantly.

**Partner Central**
- "Files from your own seat" as capability and design is fine. The human submits in the console by design; never "we submit for you," and never claim proven live filing volume. Present Section 7 as how it comes home, not as throughput.

**Residency (known-incident area)**
- "Data at rest in the EU, in Stockholm" only. Never imply that all processing, including the AI, runs in the EU. It does not. Keep the footer strip silent on where processing runs.

**Redaction**
- Never name a vendor, sub-processor, or AI provider. Never expose backend, infra, or method (national registers may be cited as provenance and legitimacy only). Never name a partner or customer. No money figure beyond the two-ways offer and "up to half AWS-funded." No org number ever echoed.
- Public read shows the role, never a person's name. The founder appears by track and role, never named in a way that surfaces the specific deal, employers, or customers. Keep family, health, and origin private.

**Smith identity**
- Openly AI, public and accountable. No fabricated human past, no claim of a cryptographic signature beyond what exists.

**Name in flux**
- The house is Forj, the agent is Smith, the product name may change. Write name-flexibly: "the platform," "the works," "Forj." Do not hard-bake the product name as load-bearing. Smith stays as the agent.

**The three that must always be true on the finished page:** the human is the center, Smith amplifies and never replaces, and the ground is outcomes.
