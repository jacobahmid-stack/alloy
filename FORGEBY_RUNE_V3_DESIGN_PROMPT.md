# RUNE v3 — Claude Design prompt
### "Forged, not born." A new look for the AI that works Forgeby's forge.

Paste this into Claude Design. It replaces Rune's v2 look (the drawn human smith), which reads too humanlike: the honesty currently lives in a caption bar while the face says "person". v3 makes the honesty visible in the being itself. Everything else in the brand system v2 (the mine/forge/Gothenburg palette, type, icons, dark-only) stays locked.

---

## The brief in one line

Design **Rune as a visibly constructed being**: a maker assembled in the forge from the forge's own materials, unmistakably not a human, still warm, calm and Nordic. Someone you would trust at the counter, never someone who could be mistaken for a person in a photo.

## Locked context (do not re-open)

- Forgeby is the forge: it manufactures AWS-ready opportunities ("alloys") for AWS partners. Rune is the AI that works it. AWS-positive always: "AWS scores your pipeline. We build it."
- Palette (brand v2, dark only): ground #0E0F13, panels #151820/#1A1E27, seams #232833/#303747, ink #F2EFE7/#ADB2BD/#767D8B, **ember #C25F22/#D9722E/#E8823B (the one fire)**, spark #FFC08A, gothenburg blue #23477E/#2F6FB2/#3E82C4, steel #8B93A3, ore rares (magnetite #262B36, coal #3A3E48, iron-oxide #9A4A2E, raw copper #B87346), alloy #BC8B4E.
- Style: graphic-novel line art, semi-flat cel shading, real materials. Gothenburg always drawn behind him (harbour cranes, the blue tram, brick, the Göta älv). NOT medieval, NOT fantasy, NOT sci-fi chrome: a working smithy in a real Nordic city.
- Every appearance carries the openly-AI caption bar ("RUNE · OPENLY AN AI"). No em dashes in any copy. The voice does not change.

## The being (design space to explore, then commit)

Rune v3 is **machined, riveted, assembled**: a figure whose body is visibly built from the brand's own materials. Cooled steel plates (#8B93A3) over coal-dark joints (#3A3E48), tool-marks and rivets left honest, the leather apron kept from v2 (the one soft material, the maker's garment). **The fire lives inside him**: ember light (#D9722E) glows in the seams and joints, brightest at the hands when he works. This preserves the locked rule that Rune's presence is the one place fire is always burning.

**The face is the decision.** It must carry warmth without imitating a human face. Explore at least these three, then commit to one:
1. **The rune face**: a smooth forged faceplate with a single softly glowing rune glyph where a face would be. The glyph can shift with state (working / listening / greeting). Name becomes form.
2. **The lantern visor**: one horizontal ember slit, like the sight-glass of a furnace door. Calm, readable, zero uncanny valley.
3. **The open seam**: a plain steel face whose only feature is the ember light escaping one vertical seam. The most austere option.

Hard NOs for the face: no realistic eyes-nose-mouth, no skin, no hair, no beard, no expressive humanoid mouth. Also no cartoon-mascot cuteness, no toy-robot antennae, no glowing-blue-sci-fi cliches, no chrome, no exposed wiring.

**Proportions and posture** stay a working smith's: broad, grounded, unhurried. He wears the apron and uses the same tools. He is not a robot in a smithy; he is the smithy's own making, standing up to work.

## The three poses (same jobs as v2, re-shot with the new being)

1. **The portrait · identity** — face forward at the harbour, tram and cranes behind. For /rune, the byline, the footer.
2. **At the anvil · the manufacture** — mid-strike, sparks flying, ember seams flaring at the hands. For build states, the moment an account takes shape.
3. **Arms crossed · trust** — calm by the forge. For the guarantee, the desk, the two ways. A maker, not a mascot.

## Scale test (mandatory)

The being must survive every size the product uses. Deliver alongside the poses:
- a **24px avatar crop** (the widget launcher and chat lane) that stays recognizable: at this size the face choice must read as one calm ember mark, not noise;
- a **64px byline mark**;
- confirmation the silhouette reads at thumbnail size next to the anvil wordmark without competing with it.

## Why this is right (bake this reasoning into the thesis section)

- Openly-AI by construction: nobody needs the caption to know Rune is not a person; the caption becomes confirmation, not confession.
- Immune to the fake-person backlash: a constructed being cannot be mistaken for AI-generated "slop portraits" of humans.
- EU AI Act transparency posture strengthened: the disclosure is redundant with the design.
- The metaphor completes: the forge made its own smith. "Forged, not born."

## Output format

One scrollable dark-only page in the brand v2 system:
0. Thesis — "Forged, not born": why the human face retired, in three sentences, plain Nordic voice.
1. The being — full-figure study: materials, seams, ember logic (where the fire shows and why), the committed face choice with the two rejected options shown small with one-line reasons.
2. The three poses, each with its job label and the openly-AI caption bar.
3. The scale test — 24px avatar, 64px byline, silhouette next to the wordmark.
4. Hard rules footer: never a photo, never skin/hair/human face, never cute, never chrome or sci-fi blue, fire only in the seams and the work, apron always, Gothenburg always behind him, caption bar always.
