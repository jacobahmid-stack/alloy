## The customer

AWS partners: the startup, the scale-up, and the AWS practice inside a bigger consultancy or SI.
(These IT firms are the BUYERS of Forj, the AWS partners. The library Forj reads is their customers,
all sectors. Never conflate the two sides.)

## The problem (verified)

An AWS partner's entire economy, co-sell engagement, tier, discounts, MDF, and the acceleration
funds, is gated on one act: originating and registering net-new customer opportunities in ACE. And
AWS scores, enriches and co-sells the pipeline a partner brings it, but it never originates the
customer list. This is on AWS's own pages: the Services tiers require launched opportunities (3 at
Select, 20 at Advanced, 50 at Premier, rolling 12 months); the June 2026 Lead Prospecting feature
scores leads a partner already uploaded rather than supplying a net-new list; and AWS said in
December 2024 that startup partners "need help generating net new business." So the load-bearing job
is manufacturing ACE-ready opportunities, and the binding constraint is top-of-funnel sourcing, not
co-sell and not delivery.

The honest line, never overclaimed: **AWS scores the list you bring it, it never hands you the list.**
("AWS does no discovery" is false; Lead Prospecting shipped in 2026.)

Per segment: the startup is AWS-documented (their own words). The AWS practice inside a house is
structurally weak at AWS-specific greenfield sourcing (Canalys puts AWS, not the SI, in front of
new-to-cloud buyers). The scale-up is a reasonable inference, not separately evidenced, so it is never
presented as proven.

## The lever (verified as a mechanism, never as a number)

The funding that wins customers attaches downstream of a named, registered opportunity. MDF and the
acceleration funds are tied to originating new opportunities: no account, no funded door. Each program
is precondition-gated and the preconditions barely overlap: MAP needs an existing estate to migrate,
PoC needs a customer not yet committed, the Partner Greenfield Program (PGP) needs a net-new account,
Activate needs a young startup. So "which funded door, if any" is a per-account classification
problem. MDF itself is a real reimbursement of up to about half the cost of third-party demand-gen
(email and telemarketing are eligible), but it must be pre-approved, fronted, and claimed in a tight
window, and fewer than a third of smaller partners have a dedicated marketing resource to run it.
Canalys (n=303) found 55% of partners rate MDF and migration funds important to closing new deals.

The money is real, precondition-gated per account, and hard to claim. Say that. Never attach a
magnitude ("60% unspent", "$X left on the table"); no primary source exists for any such figure.

## The solution

Forj creates ACE-ready opportunities for AWS partners and names the funded door each one opens. It
manufactures the net-new account from national business registers, reads its cloud from the outside,
qualifies it to AWS's own bar, names the funded play with the reasoning written, and, the capstone,
files it into the partner's own Partner Central as a partner-originated referral on a single human
approval. The whole chain, from an account AWS never handed them to an approved co-sell opportunity.

The produced outcome, defined: a held, sales-accepted meeting with a named decision-maker who has the
need and the mandate, or a filed partner-originated opportunity, pre-qualified to AWS's own validation
(Submitted, In-review, Approved) and handed over submission-ready. No-show replaced free.

## The engine

Outcomes are the ground. The human is the centre: the judgment, the send, and the close are always
theirs. Smith is the amplifier: he organises the market, finds and shapes the accounts, drafts the
opener, and packages the opportunity, and he never replaces the human. There is no send button
anywhere in him; every send and every Partner Central submission is a human's.

## The how, and the moat

Forj builds the net-new list from national registers across Europe, measures each account's cloud, and
knows market by market whether outreach is even lawful, call-first where cold email needs consent. This
is the mechanism that makes the outcome credible and hard to copy: European reach and the lawful
channel are the how, not the headline. The founder has run this exact motion, a decade of AWS partner
go-to-market in the Nordics and a major AWS partnership co-closed, in native Swedish and the terrain a
partner outside Europe cannot fake.

## Two ways to the outcome

## The competitive gap

Horizontal tools (ZoomInfo, Apollo, Clay) give contacts but have no concept of ACE, co-sell
eligibility, or which funding door an account opens. Downstream connectors (SaaSify, Tackle, Labra,
WorkSpan) and AWS's own free CRM connector transact a deal you already made. Forj is the only layer
that manufactures the net-new account, names its ACE-ready packaging and its funded next step, and
files it. Upstream creation plus the last-mile filing of Forj's own opportunities, human-gated, per
partner. Never the general-purpose connector, that lane is commoditized and AWS gives it away.
