## 1. The verdict, in one paragraph

The strategic core **survives adversarial review**: (1) **origination above the Leads page is genuinely uncontested** — verified against AWS's own what's-new pages, every AWS 2026 agent launch operates only on leads already in Partner Central, and no vendor in the entire partner-GTM category (WorkSpan, Tackle/AppDirect, Clazar, Crossbeam, PRMs) manufactures net-new opportunities; (2) **the EU-lawful supply chain is a ratchet moat** — every enforcement action against scrapers (KASPR deletion, Proxycurl shutdown, Bisnode) tightens it for free, and no US vendor can enter it retroactively; (3) **the corpus/pipeline split makes the no-signup door architecturally cheap** — the thing shown before sign-in is a shared read-only corpus needing no tenancy. The critic's one-line indictment: *every fatal risk has a specced, cheap, internally-documented fix — the danger is launching the door before those boring gates close.* So the plan below builds now, launches gated.

## 2. The two product contradictions, RESOLVED (decision, 2026-08-01)

The critic caught the thesis quietly promising both branches of two forks. Resolved now, on paper, before code:

1. **Live vs cached: the door serves HONESTLY-TIMESTAMPED NIGHTLY READS, not "live" inference.** Copy says what is true: "read last night, 03:41" — the nightly run IS the product's heartbeat and the timestamp proves the machine runs. No anonymous session ever triggers paid inference (the $900/mo cap stays safe; marginal cost of the door ≈ $0). Rune's narration is composed from shelf data, never a per-session agent run.
2. **Names vs aggregates before sign-in: AGGREGATES + BANDED POSTURE anonymous → NAMES at free sign-in → CONTACTS paid.** The anonymous door shows real counts, cloud MIX, funded-door splits for the seam, plus banded per-company posture ("runs a major cloud / no major cloud detected / workspace tenant present") on **verified-tier domains only** — never vendor-named estates of named companies from the ~62%-precise slug joins (task #30), never sole traders, never people. Free sign-in unlocks named companies. Paid unlocks contacts + claims. This maps 1:1 onto where GDPR duties begin AND onto the cost curve — the legal gate, the cost gate, and the pricing gate are the same wall.

## 3. The platform thesis (what we are building)

**One canonical tool contract; four clients.** The platform is headless-first: an MCP/API surface is the product of record, and the web door, Rune, the nightly run, and third-party agents are four clients of the same endpoints. Any feature that exists only in the UI is a bug.

- **The human door (10 seconds):** tap country chips + ICP chips → the real library answers (seam size, cloud mix, funded-door split, freshness timestamp) → "Enter the forge." Onboarding = Rune reading the visitor's actual market. Sign-in only at save/name/contact — the natural GDPR + monetization boundary. No demo mode, no sample data, ever. Research confirms this position is unoccupied: platforms gate before value (Clay, Apollo, even Lovable), utilities read instantly but aren't platforms (Ahrefs, Similarweb, HubSpot Grader). The door is both fused — and the Grader's self-qualification effect does lead-gen for free.
- **The agent door (same endpoint):** `forgeby_name_market` / `forgeby_read_company` / `forgeby_capabilities` as an open, rate-limited, watermarked remote MCP tier — every response carries attribution + the upgrade path, because **the response payload is the marketing surface**. Signed-in tools re-shell the existing internal MCP (find_prospects, cloud_and_stack, funding_fit, account_contacts with lawful-channel flags). Paid outcome tools: `reserve_alloy`, `order_dossier`, `request_meeting` (success-billed). Human-approved: `file_to_partner_central`, `draft_outreach` — the human-gate productized as trust ("your agent orders the dossier; only your human files it"). **No GTM vendor sells an outcome through MCP today** — Explorium took "agent-native data layer"; the result layer is unclaimed.
- **Discovery:** registry listings (official MCP registry, Smithery, PulseMCP, connector directories — no AWS-partner-GTM vendor is listed in any of them) — llms.txt/OpenAPI are one-day hygiene, not the channel (97% of llms.txt files get zero AI requests).
- **The positioning sentence:** *"AWS scores your pipeline. We build it."* Position explicitly ABOVE the Leads page — Forgeby fills Partner Central; AWS's free agents are the downstream that makes our output more valuable. Never pitch drafting or qualification (AWS gives both away free since Jun/Jul 2026).

## 5. The moats (ranked by durability)

1. **Outcome data co-op (the Win/Loss Foundry)** — partners demonstrably hide weak pipeline from AWS to protect tier status; the pre-ACE + suppressed-loss corpus is invisible to AWS *by partner choice*. Compounds forever. (Also finally feeds the ICP scorer real outcome signal — today's 0/144.)
2. **Territory scarcity (Mining Claims)** — only a neutral third party can sell per-seam exclusivity; AWS structurally cannot favor one partner. Solves burned accounts. **Gated on cohort proof** (see stages).
3. **The lawful-channel field + Article-14 shield** — the authority column ("blocked under UWG §7…") requires maintaining eight national legal regimes + contractual suppression-register access (SCB, CVR, Reservasjonsregisteret). A US vendor serving a 3%-revenue region will not build it; a LinkedIn-fed vendor cannot write a truthful provenance line at all.
4. **EU residency, made TRUE** — 65% of CIOs have rejected a vendor over residency; today the claim is falsifiable (91.7% of hot-path inference hits the US API; Anthropic absent from sub-processor lists). Fix is small and specced; artifact pack converts it to deal velocity.
5. **The AWS program-mechanics layer** (MAP/PoC eligibility as software) — no vendor has built it; AWS's own eligibility flags validate the pattern while staying inside its funnel.

**The re-pointed AWS watch (critic's correction, accepted):** the threat is NOT AWS building registry ETL — it's AWS **licensing third-party firmographic data (ADX rail exists) into Partner Central lead surfaces**. Free-and-dirty beats paid-and-clean for most of the market. Tripwire: any AWS/ADX announcement putting third-party company data into lead surfaces. If it fires, the story retreats to ground AWS can never take: lawful EU reachability + funded doors + held meetings. Quarterly watch also covers AppDirect acquisitions (origination completes their stack) and Clazar/Labra adding "find customers."

USD quoted, SEK invoiced. **Tier 1 $1,000/mo** (annual; unlimited seats; the doorway, not revenue; zero founder hours). **Tier 2 the Desk: greater of $10,000/mo or $1,000 per meeting held** (quarterly in advance; 3 slots published as a feature; MDF claim-pack included; $2,500 activation). ### Stage 0.6 — The forge counter (Jacob, 2026-08-01; task #39)
**The org chart, declared: Rune is the front; Jacob is the entire human layer — sales, support, and guide are one person wearing one widget.** It follows the user everywhere (door, public pages, app). Visitor mode: **Ask Rune** answers instantly, grounded in the page and the visitor's seam context (on public surfaces it obeys the open-tier + free-read rules: company-level only, origin-gated, EU inference, spend-capped); **Write to Jacob** reaches the human. Jacob mode: the SAME widget is his inbox when signed in — every thread arrives carrying the visitor's seam (country, ICP chips, what they read), so answering is founder sales with perfect context and zero CRM friction. Notification = an email ping to Jacob per human-directed message (lead-notify pattern); Slack mirror optional, never load-bearing. Honest presence ("Jacob is at the forge" / a truthful response-time line) and honest disclosure that a human may read the thread. This is the premium inversion of support — "you talk to the founder" — it matches the 3-slot desk scarcity story, it makes the no-demo door safe (nobody bounces for lack of a door handle), and it feeds the funnel: door → widget thread → held conversation is the shortest sales path the company has.
