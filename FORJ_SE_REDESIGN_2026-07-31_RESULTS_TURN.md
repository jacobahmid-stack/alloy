# forj.se redesign prompt — the results turn (2026-07-31)

**For Claude Design.** This EVOLVES the current live forj.se, it does not rebuild it. The current
site is well-made and honest; keep its craft, its integrity devices, and its dark-industrial
aesthetic. The job is to change the STORY it tells, add two missing pieces, and refresh what is
stale. Produce a single-page `.dc.html` in the same system as the current one.

---

## THE ONE CHANGE THAT DRIVES EVERYTHING

The site must rest on a strong ground of OUTCOMES, produced by a human amplified by Smith. Three
fixed truths, in this order of emphasis:

1. **The ground is outcomes.** Real results: meetings that are held, opportunities co-sold, pipeline
   that closes. The proof leads; the tool does not.
2. **The human is the centre.** The judgment, the send, and the close are always the human's. That
   never moves.
3. **Smith is the amplifier.** He organises the market, finds and shapes and drafts, and adds to what
   the human does best. He multiplies the human; he never replaces them.

**Two ways to reach the outcomes, and they have DIFFERENT reach - this is a truthfulness line, do not blur it:**
- **Run it yourself (the platform) - ALL of Europe, ANY partner.** An AWS partner anywhere in the
  world, selling into any European market, gets the whole read market: companies measured by cloud,
  the funded door named per account, the decision-makers, and the lawful-channel map for their target
  country, plus Smith to amplify THEIR OWN reps, who do the outreach in their own language and market.
  This is the honest form of "we build your European pipeline": we arm your people to build it from
  the inside. The platform is for everyone.
- **The desk (done with you) - NORDIC delivery for now, expanding.** When a partner wants the meetings
  actually delivered, Forj's people plus Smith do it with them - and this is Nordic-first today,
  because that is where the founder is natively aligned for outreach. Local SDRs are the path to other
  European markets later. It can be up to 50% AWS-funded. ⛔ DO NOT promise done-for-you meetings
  across all of Europe today; the desk is Nordic. Overclaiming this is exactly what would lose a sharp
  non-Nordic buyer.

So the page's core promise to a global AWS partner is THE PLATFORM (the read European market + lawful
channel + Smith, for their own reps, any market). The desk is the Nordic done-with-you upsell, never
the headline claim to a non-Nordic partner.

⛔ DO NOT frame Forj as a done-for-you meeting agency, and do NOT make the desk the headline. The
qualified meeting (held, sales-accepted, ACE-ready, no-show replaced) is what a human-plus-Smith
produces together; the desk is simply the option where Forj's humans do it with you. Keep the human
as the hero and Smith as the force multiplier throughout.

**The dossier definition (use where you describe what a produced outcome contains, not as the site's
headline claim):** a held, sales-accepted meeting with a named decision-maker who has the need and
the mandate, pre-qualified to AWS's own co-sell bar, handed over with the submission-ready dossier;
no-show replaced free.

## THE NEW NARRATIVE SPINE (section order)

1. **Hero — the result.** Headline is the meeting, not the tool. Something in the register of "The
   meeting AWS will co-sell, booked for you" or "We forge the meeting. You close the deal." Keep the
   live library unit-chart below it (it is honest and it is proof of scale). Keep "invite-only, data
   at rest in the EU."

2. **What you get — the meeting-ready dossier.** A short, concrete section naming the four things
   every delivered meeting carries: the named decision-maker with the mandate; the measured business
   need (the cloud/estate signal); the funded AWS play named, never guessed (MAP / Activate / PoC);
   and the meeting pre-qualified to the AWS co-sell bar so it is submission-ready. End on the promise:
   held and sales-accepted, or replaced free.

3. **Watch it work — the live read.** KEEP the current live read almost as-is. It is the machine
   producing the raw material of a dossier, shown honestly (role not name, one clock, empty rows
   explain themselves, org number never echoed back). It is the strongest proof device on the page.

4. **Two ways to work with us.** A first-class fork, not a footnote:
   - **Done with you (the desk).** Forj's desk delivers the qualified meetings. This is the result
     product. Note it can be up to 50% AWS-funded (MDF), framed as the offer, never the mechanics.
   - **Run it yourself (the platform).** The same machine in the partner's own workspace, for partners
     who staff the outreach themselves.
   Make the choice obvious and calm. This IS the offer; showing how a customer engages is allowed.

5. **Who forges your pipeline — the founder section (NEW, this is the desk's credibility).** Copy
   below, verbatim intent, GENERIC names (do not print employer or deal names). A short bio plus one
   line that makes the language the wedge.

6. **The machine — how the meeting is produced.** Condense the current 8-station journey into a
   tighter "how the work happens" band: the night shift (registry sweep, rescore, cloud reads,
   signals, the Letter, the brief), the funded doors, and Partner Central where the deal comes home.
   This is the current site's best explanatory material; keep it, but as the HOW behind the result,
   not the main event.

7. **Smith's signed identity + the human-approval integrity.** KEEP unchanged in substance: openly an
   AI, a public signed identity page, no send button anywhere, every send is a human's from their own
   inbox, and the "File to Partner Central" approval that ships visibly inert in every demo.

8. **The invite / call to action.** Keep calm and invite-only.

## UPDATE WHAT IS STALE

- **Geography.** The shelf is no longer Sweden/Norway/Finland with "the UK loaded next." It now spans
  the Nordics plus France, Ireland and Belgium, and is growing across Europe. Retire "the United
  Kingdom is loaded next" as the single next step; say the reading now reaches into Europe, with more
  markets opening. IMPORTANT: the live public statistics are deliberately scoped to the countries with
  full cloud coverage, so change the COPY freely but do not hand-write country counts that the live
  stats do not yet back. Let the baked numbers stay the source of truth.
- **The roadmap band** ("the United Kingdom and France open on the shelf") is now partly shipped;
  refresh it to what is genuinely still coming.

## ELEVATE WHAT IS NOW REAL

- **The lawful-channel feature.** The current line "where cold email is not allowed, the play routes
  to a call first" is now a live capability across fourteen countries. Make it a named differentiator:
  Forj knows, market by market, whether you may email a named person or must call first. Denmark and
  Germany are call-first markets, not dead ones. No competitor ships this. Frame it as the outreach
  that obeys the local rule, so a global partner never trips a European law they did not know existed.

## GUARDRAILS (do not violate — these are the spine of the brand)

- **No em dashes anywhere.**
- **ONE Smith, one place.** Smith's face appears exactly ONCE on the page, where he truly belongs
  (his identity moment). Do NOT scatter his portrait across sections with different faces (the current
  site uses five separate Smith images, which cheapens him). Reference him by name elsewhere; picture
  him once. Keep it clean.
- **Redaction:** never name a vendor, sub-processor, or AI provider; never expose backend, infra, or
  methods; never show business economics beyond the offer (the two ways to engage IS allowed); never
  name a partner or customer.
- **Truthfulness on residency:** "data at rest in the EU / Stockholm" is fine. Do NOT imply that all
  processing, including the AI, runs in the EU. It does not. Keep any residency claim to data at rest.
- **The read's red lines:** the public read shows the ROLE, never a person's name; no money figure;
  the organisation number is never rendered back.
- **Buyer language, not engineer jargon.** Outcomes, never the how-it-is-built.
- **Voice:** earned-not-given, relentless but anchored in work and craft, never hustle-culture. "Forj
  = the forge = you earn your place."
- **Aesthetic:** dark industrial steel and ember, with violet as the single restrained accent. If it
  can be done without breaking the system, bring the violet-accent light/dark satellite pages
  (pricing, legal) and the dark homepage into one coherent seam, but do not sacrifice the homepage's
  character to do it.
- **Integrity devices to keep:** the inert Partner Central approval, the "sample data" flag when the
  live read is unreachable, the reconcile guard that refuses to show numbers that do not sum.

## WHAT TO PRESERVE OUTRIGHT (the current site's best work)

The live interactive read; the honest unit-chart hero; the night-shift timeline; the funded-doors
explanation; Smith's signed identity; the human-approves-every-send promise; the live-baked statistics
with the reconcile guard; the calm invite-only tone. The redesign keeps all of these and reorders the
story around the result.

## AFTER CLAUDE DESIGN RETURNS

The `.dc.html` comes back to the build pipeline: `tools/convert-redesign.mjs` transforms it,
`tools/build.mjs` bakes the live stats, strips comments, and runs the 24-rule leak scrub on the served
bytes (the build FAILS on any leak). Then sync `dist` to the deploy target and push. Nothing reaches
the public site that the scrub has not cleared.
