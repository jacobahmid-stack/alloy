# FORGEBY BRAND SYSTEM v2 — Claude Design prompt
### "Find the color system in the mine, the forge, and Gothenburg."

Paste this into Claude Design. It refines the v1 brand system (which was strong on character but used the wrong fonts and a light set we are dropping). The job is to **find the definitive Forgeby color system by pulling every color from a real place in Rune's world** — and to correct the type. Produce one scrollable, dark-only brand-system page.

---

## Locked context (do not re-open)

- **Forgeby** is the company AND the platform: the forge. **Rune** is the AI that works it. Rune **mines** the raw account and **forges** it into an **alloy** (lowercase common noun) = an AWS-ready opportunity. AWS scores and funds the alloy. The partner closes it.
- **AWS-positive, always.** The line is "AWS scores your pipeline. We build it." Then AWS funds it, you close it. Never frame AWS by what it does not do. Never negative, never indifferent. Warm ally.
- **Rune is always DRAWN** (graphic-novel line art, semi-flat cel shading, real materials: leather, iron, fire, stone, water), **openly an AI** (every appearance carries the openly-AI caption bar), never a photo, never cropped to pass as a person. Three poses, each one job: **portrait/identity**, **at-the-anvil/manufacture**, **arms-crossed/trust**. Gothenburg is always drawn behind him.
- **Icons are custom line marks from Rune's world**: the anvil, the hammer, the spark, the tram, the crane, the Göta älv bridge. The blue lion is a drawn crest chip only, never redrawn as a line icon. Two-pixel line marks, **steel by default, ember only when active** (the spark is the one mark that defaults to ember).

## Two corrections from v1 (non-negotiable)

1. **TYPE.** Display AND body = **Familjen Grotesk** (one humanist-industrial face, Swedish-designed for a Gothenburg company; set large and quiet). Technical/labels/data/numerals = **Martian Mono**, whose **width axis is the signature** (font-stretch 75% condensed for labels and eyebrows, 112.5% expanded for big numerals; tabular-nums everywhere). **Drop Space Grotesk, Inter, and Spline Sans Mono entirely.** They are the default pairing of every AI startup and are retired.
2. **ONE GROUND.** Dark-only. **Drop the light "cream worktop" set.** The product lives at night, in the mine and at the forge.

---

## The job: the color system, from THREE real sources

Do not invent colors off a mood board. Pull every token from a real place in Rune's world. Three sources, one system:

**1. THE MINE — the raw material and the deep dark.** Where the ore comes from before any fire. This is the **story of the grounds** and the reason the product is dark: you are at depth, in the raw material, before the forge. Pull the grounds and the deepest surfaces from the mine: blue-steel black (never pure black), ore and mineral in the dark (iron ore / magnetite, coal, slag, raw copper-in-rock). The mine also yields the **ore/earth rares** (copper, iron-oxide, brass, coal-grey) used sparingly.

**2. THE FORGE — the heat that works the ore.** **Ember (~#D9722E family) = the one action color.** Forge fire, hot iron, the leather apron. Ember strikes **once** per view, on the action (the CTA, the active state, the lit operative verb "forge/build").

**3. GOTHENBURG — the place it all happens.** **Gothenburg blue (~#2F6FB2 family) = links and the second brand color** (the tram, the blue lion). Plus the rare place-tones used sparingly: copper roofs / patina, brick facades, the flag yellow (only where it truly belongs). **Steel grey (~#8B93A3) = structural neutral and the default icon color.**

**Deliver named color roles** — ground, panel/raised, hairline, ink ramp (3 steps), ember action (3 steps), Gothenburg-blue link (3 steps), steel neutral / icon-default, ore/mineral rares, and the functional cloud-data colors (AWS / Azure / Google / independent / unread) — each with exact hex, dark-only, all AA+ for body text. **Show the derivation visibly**: a color-story strip that traces each token back to the mine, the forge, or Gothenburg.

## Hard rules (must hold)

- **NO VIOLET anywhere.** It is retired. It is nowhere in Rune's world.
- **Ember is the accent, not the field.** Mostly ground and steel; ember strikes once.
- **Separate ember from AWS amber.** Ember #D9722E (forge fire, redder and deeper) must be visibly distinct from **AWS amber #D98A33, which is a functional DATA color only**. AWS amber appears ONLY as the AWS cloud-data chip: never a CTA, never adjacent to an ember action. Use the mine's ore/earth-tones to widen the warm family so ember reads as one deliberate fire, not a lone orange colliding with AWS. If the two still read too alike, shift ember's hue until the roles are unmistakable.
- **Gothenburg blue is the second color and must earn its moment** (links, selection, one blue section heading).
- **No em dashes in any copy.** The voice is Nordic and plain: state the fact and stop.
- **The wordmark:** FORGEBY, Familjen Grotesk, uppercase, letter-spaced, with the anvil mark left, ember on dark. Never violet, never a gradient fill.

## The metaphor the palette must be able to tell

mine (raw ore, deep ground) → Rune forges it (ember heat) → the alloy (finished warm metal, the AWS-ready opportunity) → AWS scores and funds it (the assay) → the partner closes it. A page should tell this with color alone: **dark mine grounds, one ember strike on the action, Gothenburg blue on the links that connect it, steel on the tools and icons.**

## Output format

One scrollable, dark-only brand-system page, same spirit as v1, corrected:
0. **Thesis** — one smith (Rune), one city (Gothenburg), the palette pulled off the mine, the forge, and the tram, not a mood board. Violet retired.
1. **Wordmark + anvil mark** — Familjen Grotesk, ember mark on dark and on any rare surface; never violet, never gradient.
2. **Rune, drawn** — the three poses, each labelled with its job, each with the openly-AI caption bar. Not medieval, not fantasy: a working smith.
3. **THE COLOR SYSTEM** — the mine / forge / Gothenburg derivation, every named token with hex, role, source and AA note. This is the centerpiece.
4. **Type** — Familjen Grotesk (display + body) and Martian Mono (technical), three roles, with the width-axis samples.
5. **The icon motif set** — Rune's world as two-pixel line marks, steel default / ember active.
6. **Applied** — one dark hero: the anvil mark, "AWS scores your pipeline. We build it." (the verb lit ember), an ember CTA "Name your market", a Gothenburg-blue link "Walk through the works", a mono label; plus the manufacture moment (an account taking shape on the anvil). Hard-rules footer.
